About
Royale Pilates

Royale Pilates is a PREMIER, FULLY EQUIPPED pilates studio dedicated to delivering a comprehensive Pilates experience. We provide access to the full spectrum of equipment, including Reformer, Cadillac, Barrel, Chair, and the Spine Corrector, ensuring a well-rounded approach to movement and rehabilitation.



We offer private, duet, and group classes, catering to individuals of all fitness levels and ages, from 6 to 80 years old. What sets us apart is our specialized expertise, beyond general fitness Pilates, we provide tailored programs for posture correction, scoliosis, kids' Pilates, injury prevention, and prenatal and postnatal care. 

Private Pilates Sessions

Our one-on-one sessions are fully customized to your body, goals, and experience level. With focused guidance and access to the full range of Pilates equipment, you’ll receive a deeply personalized experience that supports strength, mobility, and mindful movement. Ideal for rehabilitation, specialized conditions, or anyone seeking a more targeted approach.
1:1 Private Class

Personalized Pilates sessions designed around your body, goals, and pace. Enjoy focused attention to refine technique, build strength, and improve mobility.

1:2 Duet Class

A semi-private Pilates session for two, offering personalized attention while sharing the experience with a partner or friend. Ideal for those who enjoy motivation, support, and a touch of friendly fun while working towards individual goals.
